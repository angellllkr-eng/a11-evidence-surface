## Evidence standard

A repository being public does not by itself prove production deployment, commercial traction, investment, recognition, or customer adoption.

For each material claim, the portfolio distinguishes:

- **Verified** — supported by a concrete repository, commit, pull request, workflow result, release, or other inspectable record.
- **Ready** — prepared for the next verification gate.
- **Unverified** — not presented as established fact.
- **Historical** — retained for provenance but not treated as current authority.

## Founder / investment-program review

For accelerators, investment programmes, strategic partners, and institutional reviewers, the portfolio is designed to make the underlying work inspectable rather than relying on promotional claims.

Reviewers can trace:

1. the product or system,
2. its canonical repository,
3. architecture and implementation,
4. engineering activity,
5. validation evidence,
6. security and governance controls,
7. current versus historical sources.

## Operating principle

**Build useful systems. Make authority explicit. Prove what works. Protect what matters. Keep the record.**

This public surface is intentionally evidence-led. Private repositories and owner-control systems remain separate and are not represented as public capabilities unless there is a verified public record.
